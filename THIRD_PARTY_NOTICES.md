# Landing page credits

- The Moon renderer uses the [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/) color and elevation maps from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio. The still image is rendered from the same maps. Their visual lighting and orientation are illustrative.
- The poster sections adapt the [Astro Association template](https://21st.dev/@kedhareswer/library/astro-association) by Kedhareswer Naidu. The supplied template archive specifies the MIT license.
