# Design

## Direction

An evidence workbench: a quiet, pale blue frame surrounds a flat research sheet. Experimental values and source identifiers carry the visual emphasis.

## Typography

IBM Plex Sans for interface and prose; IBM Plex Mono for source identifiers. Local font files are preferred; system sans is a fallback. Body 14–16px, line-height 1.6; headings 22–32px. Tabular numbers for measurements.

## Tokens

Ground #eef2f3, surface #fdfefe, text #17282f, muted #596c75, border #dce4e7, teal #146477, warm outcome mark #a55c28. No gradients, shadows, invented metrics, NASA logos, or decorative imagery.

## Layout

228px sidebar, 52px top bar, 24–32px content padding. Two-column investigation catalogue with real metadata. Data tables scroll within their own region. At 760px navigation becomes a horizontally scrollable bar and content becomes one column.

## Interaction

Every control has visible focus. Search, filter, comparison and export are available with the keyboard. Empty, loading and error states use plain instructions. Chart points open the exact source record; a tabular alternative accompanies charts.

## Content

Name units, distinguish initial ambient oxygen from burner oxygen, expose missing measurements, and cite source files/rows. SI-generated summaries are marked. No risk score or safety certification.
